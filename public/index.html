For iPhone, iPad and Mac. Android is next.

Hear the next track before the room does.

Soundboard plays the speakers on one output and your headphones on another. Tap Mix and the next track lands on the beat, on the phrase. Turn the phone sideways when you want jog wheels, pitch and loops.

StartDocs

Version 0.2.1 · 2026-11-07 · draft · internal

Starts with one button. Opens up when you do.

Quick

Phone upright. One deck, the next track suggested by tempo, key and energy, and a Mix button that blends it in on the next phrase.

Mix

Sideways or on a Mac. Both decks as scrolling waveforms, EQ, filter, hot cues, sync and the crossfader.

DJ

Jog wheels that scratch like vinyl, pitch faders, nudge, loops from a quarter beat to 32, and echo. Tap any BPM for pitch and sync.

Visuals for the room

The visualizer reads the master output, so it moves with exactly what the speakers play. Spectrum, Radial, Scope or Pulse, in your colours.

On iPhone and iPad it takes over a connected display or an Apple TV over AirPlay while the decks stay on your phone. On a Mac it opens in its own window for a second screen.

The headphones never reach the room

Built for the booth

A real cue on whatever you plug in
Splitter cable, USB interface, DJ controller, or two outputs on a Mac. Where cueing is impossible, it says so and still syncs and mixes.
One engine everywhere
A C++ engine does every sample on every platform: sync, analysis, limiters, routing and the vinyl platter. 32 tests run plain and under sanitizers.
Sets that pick up where you left off
Named sessions keep decks, mixer and queue. Cue points and loops stay with each track. The last session reopens when you do.
Your look
Obsidian by default: true black on OLED and spectral waveforms. Theme, accent, deck view and waveform colours live in Settings.

Start

make setup
make validate
make dev

On a Mac, make dev opens the Xcode project; run the Soundboard scheme. Local development covers Android and the engine.

For agents

Start at llm.txt, then AGENTS.md and Status. There is no MCP server or HTTP API; the engine's interface is its C API.
