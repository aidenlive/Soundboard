# 0004 · Split is the primary mark; Pulse is a supporting element

Status · Accepted · 2026-11-04

## Context

Four brandmark directions were drawn on the canvas: Ring, Split, Pulse and Crossfade.

## Decision

Split is the mark: two half-discs, white for master and lime for cue, offset so together they read as an S. Pulse (bars on an S-curve) is kept as a supporting element meaning that sound is happening; it is never a button or a logo.

## Consequences

- The mark explains the product's core idea, two buses.
- Pulse appears in loading and activity states only.
- The name is a working name; trademark clearance is open.
