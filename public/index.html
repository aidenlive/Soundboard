DJ app · iPhone · Android · Mac

Mix two tracks in seconds. Hear the next one first.

Soundboard plays the room on one output and your headphones on another, on a phone, a tablet or a Mac. Tap Mix and it syncs the next track and blends it in on the phrase. When you want control, the same app opens up to EQ, hot cues, loops and three-band waveforms.

What it does

A real cue on any hardware it can find
Splitter cable, USB interface, DJ controller, or two devices on a Mac. Where cueing is impossible (a phone alone, Bluetooth) it says so and still syncs and mixes for you.
Mixes on the phrase
Blend, bass swap, filter sweep or echo out, timed to the outgoing track's next 16-beat phrase. Up Next ranks your library by tempo, key and energy.
One engine everywhere
A C++ engine does every sample on every platform: sync, analysis (BPM, key, loudness), limiters, routing and the vinyl platter. 32 tests run plain and under sanitizers.
Scratch when you want to
Turn the phone sideways and switch to DJ: two jog wheels that scratch like vinyl, pitch faders, loops, cues and echo. The default screen stays one Mix button.
Visuals for the room
A real-time visualizer of the master output, full screen on the device, on an external display or Apple TV, or in its own Mac window.
Your look
Obsidian by default: true black on OLED and spectral waveforms. Change theme, accent, deck view and waveform colours in Settings.

Start

make setup
make validate
make dev

On a Mac, make dev opens the Xcode project; run the Soundboard scheme. Local development covers Android and the engine.

For agents

Start at llm.txt, then AGENTS.md and Status. There is no MCP server or HTTP API; the engine's interface is its C API.
